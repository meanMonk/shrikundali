# ShriKundali — 6-Week Ad Campaign Plan

One product angle per week. Same engine, same checkout, different landing page + creative. Budget suggestion: ₹1,000/day per campaign, split 2-3 creatives, optimize for Purchase (not clicks).

| Week | Product | Landing Page | Price | Platform |
|------|---------|--------------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Money Kundali | `/` | ₹199 | Meta |
| 2 | Marriage Timing | `/p/marriage-kundali` | ₹199 | Meta + Google |
| 3 | Job vs Business (Career) | `/p/career-kundali` | ₹199 | Meta |
| 4 | Dosha Report | `/p/dosha-report` | ₹199 | Google Search |
| 5 | Kundali Matching | `/p/kundali-matching` | ₹299 | Meta |
| 6 | Health Kundali | `/p/health-kundali` | ₹199 | Meta |

Don't move to the next week's product until the current one has a clean 3-5 day read on cost-per-purchase. If a week's angle wins, spend the following week making 5-10 creative variants of it instead of moving on (per PRD strategy: double down on winners).

---

## Flyer / creative image prompts (for AI image generation)

Style baseline for all flyers: **modern Vedic-astrology aesthetic — deep indigo/maroon gradient background, gold line-art mandala or chart motif, clean sans-serif Hindi+English type, mobile-first 4:5 or 9:16 ratio, no clutter, one clear headline, one CTA button.**

### Week 1 — Money Kundali
"A mobile-first Instagram ad flyer, 4:5 ratio, deep indigo background with subtle gold mandala pattern, a glowing golden rupee coin overlapping a minimal Vedic birth chart (South Indian square kundali diagram) in gold line art, bold white/gold Devanagari-friendly headline text 'Bank Balance Kab Double Hoga?', small subtext 'Your Money Kundali in 2 minutes', a gold CTA button reading 'Check Now', clean modern astrology-app aesthetic, no photorealistic faces, flat vector illustration style."

### Week 2 — Marriage Timing
"A mobile-first Instagram ad flyer, 4:5 ratio, deep maroon-to-rose gradient background, two minimal gold line-art wedding rings intertwined above a small Vedic kundali chart icon, bold headline 'Shaadi Kab Hogi?', subtext 'Find your most favorable marriage window', gold CTA button 'Check Now', elegant festive but not cluttered, flat vector illustration style."

### Week 3 — Job vs Business
"A mobile-first Instagram ad flyer, 4:5 ratio, deep navy background with gold constellation lines, a split visual — briefcase icon on one side, growth-chart/rocket icon on the other, connected by a subtle gold Vedic chart in the center, bold headline 'Job Karu Ya Business?', subtext 'Your Kundali has the answer', gold CTA button 'Get Report', clean flat vector illustration style."

### Week 4 — Dosha Report
"A mobile-first Instagram ad flyer, 4:5 ratio, dark plum background with soft gold rays, a protective shield line-art icon overlaying a small Vedic kundali chart, bold headline 'Kya Aapki Kundali Mein Dosh Hai?', subtext 'Manglik, Kaal Sarp & more — with remedies', gold CTA button 'Check Now', calm reassuring tone, flat vector illustration style, no scary imagery."

### Week 5 — Kundali Matching
"A mobile-first Instagram ad flyer, 4:5 ratio, warm maroon-gold gradient, two minimal gold birth-chart squares side by side connecting with a glowing gold link/heart in the middle, bold headline 'Yeh Rishta Sahi Hai Ya Nahi?', subtext 'Guna Milan Score in minutes', gold CTA button 'Check Compatibility', elegant flat vector illustration style."

### Week 6 — Health Kundali
"A mobile-first Instagram ad flyer, 4:5 ratio, deep teal-indigo gradient background, a minimal gold line-art lotus or pulse-line icon overlapping a small Vedic kundali chart, bold headline 'Health Ke Sanket Kya Kehte Hain?', subtext 'Preventive guidance from your birth chart', gold CTA button 'Get Report', calm wellness tone, flat vector illustration style."

## Weekly checklist
1. Swap landing page headline/CTA already done in code (see `packages/web/kundaliweb/src/pages/`).
2. Generate 2-3 flyer variants from the prompt above (try different headline phrasing per variant).
3. Pair with 1 short (15-20s) Reel using the matching hook from `meta-ads-copy.md`.
4. Launch at ₹1,000/day, check Purchase cost after 3 days, kill or scale.
