# Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads Copy — ShriKundali

Source: Meta Ad Library scrape (Sep 2026) of Indian astrology advertisers (AstroTime, Astroyogi, Reva, Dharmayana, Aditya Kundali, etc.) + PRD strategy doc.

Pattern that works across the market: **Hinglish, emotional hook, DOB-based curiosity, "first chat/report free", instant answer.** We are not doing free chat — we sell an instant PDF report — so our hook must promise instant clarity, not a call.

Each ad below maps to one landing page in `packages/web/kundaliweb/src/pages/`.

## Notes
- Keep disclaimer line in every ad's comment/landing page: "Astrological guidance, not financial/medical/legal advice."
- Video ads: 15-20s, mobile vertical, same hook as primary text spoken in Hindi/Hinglish over on-screen captions (matches how AstroTime/Reva structure their winning creatives).
