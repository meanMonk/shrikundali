## 1. Headline finding: the premium report a paying customer receives is broken

Rendered both PDFs to PNG at each key section and cross-checked against `packages/backend/kundaliapi/src/lib/render.ts` (the actual generator for the 58-page file). On this real order (`TeaMkMFBnr228X`):

| Section | What it should show | What it actually shows |
|---|---|---|
| Cover page | Branding, name, birth details (teaser does this well) | Plain black serif text: just `sahil`, no logo, no color, no birth summary strip |
| Lagna (Ascendant) | e.g. "Vrishchik" | **`—`** (blank) |
| "Your Ascendant is X, ruled by Y" paragraph | Full sentence | **"Your Ascendant is —, a sign ruled by —."** |
| Planetary Positions table | 9 rows (Sun..Ketu) | **0 rows — header only** |
| Per-planet paragraphs (Sun in X, House Y...) | 9 paragraphs | **Absent** (loop is empty because `planets` array is empty) |
| House-by-House Analysis (12 houses) | Real sign + lord per house | All 12 houses show **"falls in —, making — the lord"** |
| Personalised Remedies | 9 remedy cards | **Completely empty section** — jumps straight from Money-Axis to Conclusion |
| Money-Axis Scores (6 axes) | Computed 1–10 scores | **Every single axis defaulted to flat 5/10 "Moderate"** |
| Yogas / Doshas / Dasha (mahadasha-level) | Real data | ✅ These are fine — real yoga descriptions, real dosha text, real dasha date ranges |

Root cause (visible directly in `render.ts:196-217, 234-238`): `buildPlanets()` reads `d.planet_positions` and looks for a row named `"ascendant"`. For this order, `planet_positions` came back empty (or without an ascendant row) from the data source, and **nothing downstream guards against that** — `asc` becomes `undefined`, `ascSignId` becomes `0`, and that zero cascades into all 12 houses' sign/lord lookups, the whole planetary table, and the remedies loop, silently. Yogas/doshas/dasha are unaffected because they're read from separate top-level fields (`yoga_details`, `mangal_dosha`, `dasha_periods`) that didn't fail.

This is very likely a real instance of the exact risk your `report.ts`/`prokerala.ts` `ProkeralaQuotaError` fallback path exists for (see `2026-09-21-payment-report-implementation.md`) — a degraded/partial ProKerala response got rendered and **delivered to a paying customer** as a full 58-page PDF with no validation catching it.

**This is the #1 fix, above anything cosmetic.** A customer who just saw the polished ₹199 teaser and paid for "60+ page Premium" would receive a report where the two most personal, most-quoted numbers (Lagna, planetary table) are blank, and every money score is an identical, meaningless "5/10 Moderate" — worse than not sending a report at all.

## 2. Second headline finding: 41 of the 58 pages are unreadable dasha noise

Mapped every page to its section header:

- Pages 1–4: cover, birth details, panchang, avakahada chakra
- Pages 5–12: Lagna/Rashi analysis, planetary positions, house-by-house, yogas, doshas
- **Pages 13–53 (41 pages, ~71% of the report): raw Pratyantardasha tables**, one table per antardasha, generated for **every mahadasha across the person's full ~120-year Vimshottari cycle** — the dump runs out to sub-period dates in **the year 2111**.
- Pages 54–58: money-axis, remedies (empty), conclusion

`render.ts:506-520` has an explicit, deliberate loop: *"Full pratyantardasha detail for every antardasha in this mahadasha"* — this runs unconditionally for all 9 mahadashas, not just the current/upcoming one. No paying customer reads 40+ pages of "Mercury/Ketu sub-period: 04 Sept 2040 → 22 Sept 2040" repeated hundreds of times, decades past what's relevant to them today. This is the single biggest reason the premium report *feels* unsellable next to the tight 13-page teaser — it's not that we lack content, it's that 70% of the page count is noise drowning the genuinely good sections (yogas, doshas, money-axis).

**Fix:** restrict pratyantardasha detail to the *current* mahadasha/antardasha only (maybe +1 upcoming), and give every other mahadasha just its antardasha-level table (which is already there and is the right amount of detail). This alone should cut the report from ~58 pages to something like 15–20 — a much more premium-feeling length, not "more pages = better."

## 3. What the teaser (`sambhai-kundali.pdf`) is doing right, worth carrying into the premium report

Since this is genuinely our own well-designed product, it's the right reference point:

- Dark navy/gold cover with logo, birth-details strip (lagna/rashi/nakshatra/mool/bhagya at a glance) — the premium report's cover is currently just a name in serif type with zero branding.
- Table of contents on page 2.
- Per-planet **dignity/strength** callouts and a single "weakest planet — needs the most attention" page — a good compact synthesis the premium report doesn't have (it has 9 full paragraphs instead of one prioritized callout).
- Numerology page.
- A dense "at a glance" summary table.
- Consolidated warnings/remedies page.

None of these depend on chart-image rendering (donut charts, North/South Indian diamonds) — I searched this codebase and found **no chart-drawing code anywhere** (`render.ts`'s `Block` model only supports `h1/h2/h3/p/ul/table/pagebreak` — text and tables, no SVG/canvas). The earlier review's claim that our premium PDF already has "money chakra donut," "North Indian diamond," "Rashi degree wheel" charts is **not true of this code path** — that was likely describing the teaser's design language, misattributed. If actual chart graphics are wanted in the premium PDF, that's still new work, not something to verify as "already shipped."

## 4. Priority order

1. **Stop shipping broken data** — add a guard in `render.ts`/`report.ts`: if `buildPlanets()` returns no ascendant or an empty `planet_positions`, fail the render loudly (retry / alert / block delivery) instead of producing a 58-page PDF full of `—` and flat 5/10 scores. This is the smoke test the earlier review already recommended (§2 there) — it's not hypothetical, it already happened on order `TeaMkMFBnr228X`.
2. **Cut the dasha section down** — pratyantardasha detail only for the current (± next) mahadasha/antardasha, not all 9. This is the highest-leverage length/usability fix in the whole report.
3. **Give the premium PDF the teaser's visual identity** — branded dark/gold cover, TOC, birth-details-at-a-glance strip. Right now the premium report a customer pays for looks visually worse than the free-adjacent teaser that sold it.
4. **Add a compact "weakest planet" / at-a-glance summary page**, similar to the teaser's, ahead of the 9 full planet paragraphs — gives readers the synthesis before the detail.
5. Only after 1–3: consider new chart graphics (donut, diamond charts, Navamsa) — real, worthwhile additions, but secondary to fixing broken data and the page-count problem.
